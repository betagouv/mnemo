# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

## Context

What problem requires a decision? Which constraints and quality attributes
matter? State facts separately from assumptions.

## Options considered

- **Option A:** benefit, cost, and reason to choose or reject it.
- **Option B:** benefit, cost, and reason to choose or reject it.

## Decision

State the chosen approach and its scope precisely. Say what it does **not**
decide if that boundary could be misunderstood.

## Consequences

- **Benefits:** what becomes easier or possible.
- **Costs and risks:** what becomes harder, more expensive, or dependent on
  another system.
- **Follow-up:** implementation or migration work that remains.

## Validation and review

What evidence supports the choice? How will it be tested, and what would cause
the decision to be revisited?
